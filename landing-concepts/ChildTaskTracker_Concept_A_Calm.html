How it works

Three quiet steps, every day.

Each task carries its own soft colour — a private visual language your family learns without ever thinking about it.

✓

Check it off

Your child marks a task done from their own simple list — teeth, homework, lunch, bedtime. No timers, no pressure.

◔

Confirm with a glance

You get one soft notification. Check in, tap approve. If life gets busy, it approves itself after a day.

✦

Watch the week fill in

Points build quietly toward rewards you've already agreed on — never a surprise, never a negotiation.
